# Appendix A — Infrared Consistency of Emergent General Relativity

## A.0 Purpose and Scope
This appendix documents the conditions under which an emergent Lorentzian continuum
description governed by the Einstein field equations arises as a consistent infrared
effective theory from a discrete causal optimization substrate.

The goal is conditional consistency, not fundamental derivation.

---

## A.1 Discrete Event-State Structure
EST postulates a sequence of discrete global states \( \{ \Psi_n \} \),
indexed by an ordering parameter without assuming fundamental time.

Each state is associated with a directed acyclic causal graph.

---

## A.2 Emergent Temporal Ordering
The ordering of event states induces a foliation-like structure in the continuum limit.
This ordering parameter functions as an emergent time coordinate without being fundamental.

---

## A.3 Locality and Bounded Propagation
Finite update stencils impose bounded causal propagation per step.
In the coarse-grained limit, this induces effective null cones and causal structure.

---

## A.4 Coarse-Graining and Diffeomorphism Invariance
Block averaging over many events renders vertex relabeling physically irrelevant.
In the continuum description, this manifests as diffeomorphism invariance.

---

## A.5 Metric Description
Introduce an effective metric:
\[
g_{\mu\nu} = \eta_{\mu\nu} + h_{\mu\nu}.
\]

Directional anisotropies couple at leading order to connection-level quantities
(first derivatives of the metric perturbation).

---

## A.6 Effective Action
Assuming locality and second-order field equations, the effective geometric action
must take the Einstein–Hilbert form in four dimensions:
\[
S = \int d^4x \sqrt{-g} (R - 2\Lambda).
\]

This follows from Lovelock’s theorem once the Lorentzian continuum description is established.

---

## A.7 Field Equations
Variation with respect to the metric yields the Einstein field equations:
\[
G_{\mu\nu} + \Lambda g_{\mu\nu} = 8\pi G_{\mathrm{eff}} T_{\mu\nu}.
\]

---

## A.8 Parameter Matching
The effective Newton constant depends on microscopic parameters such as
local penalty strength and characteristic spacing.

Exact matching requires model-specific analysis.

---

## A.9 Explicit Assumptions
- Existence of a continuum limit
- Locality of the effective description
- Absence of higher-derivative instabilities

---

## A.10 Explicit Non-Claims
This appendix does not:
- Derive GR from fundamental genesis principles
- Assert uniqueness of spacetime emergence
- Fix numerical values of coupling constants
