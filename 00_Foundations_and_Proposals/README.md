# Foundations & Proposals
**The Conceptual Architecture of Event-State Theory**

This directory contains the foundational texts and forward-looking experimental proposals that frame the EST research program. Unlike the formal results in `01_Publications`, these documents outline the philosophical origins, the complete narrative synthesis, and the falsifiable predictions of the theory.

## 📄 Documents

### 1. The Event-State Protocols (The Monograph)
*   **File:** `EST_Monograph_Original.pdf`
*   **Description:** The original manuscript defining the axioms of Discrete Causal Frames, the Cost Function ($J$), and the concept of "Scar Tissue" ($\epsilon$). This is the origin story of the framework.

### 2. The Foundational Synthesis (Whitepaper)
*   **File:** `EST_Foundational_Synthesis.pdf`
*   **Description:** An executive summary connecting the theory's axioms to its major results: the resolution of the Hubble Tension, the emergence of the Cosmic Web, and the derivation of General Relativity. **Start here for a high-level overview.**

### 3. The LHC Experimental Proposal
*   **File:** `LHC_PCR_Experimental_Proposal.pdf`
*   **Description:** A detailed proposal for detecting **Pre-Causal Resonance (PCR)** in high-energy collider data. It outlines the specific time-window ($t < 0$) and energy signatures required to falsify the theory using CMS/ATLAS datasets.
